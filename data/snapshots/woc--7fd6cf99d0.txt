Congress information
The
World Ophthalmology Congress® (WOC®)
, established with its first International Congress of Ophthalmology in 1857, is the oldest continuing international medical congress. It addresses all subspecialties and related interests in ophthalmology.
It is organized by the
International Council of Ophthalmology
(ICO)
and brings together thousands of eye care professionals from around the world.
Reasons to attend
Discover why the World Ophthalmology Congress 2026 is a must-attend event for eye care professionals worldwide:
Engage with scientific program content across all subspecialties
Gain insights from global leaders through expert-led sessions
Network with international peers and industry leaders
Explore the latest innovations at an expansive exhibition
Earn CME credits through accredited courses and workshops
Participate in panel discussions and contribute to ICO initiatives

Location & venue
Join us in the heart of Europe at the Prague Congress Centre – an exceptional venue in one of the world’s most enchanting cities.
Overlooking the historic cityscape, the Centre offers state-of-the-art facilities designed for seamless learning and collaboration. Just minutes from Prague’s iconic landmarks, attendees will enjoy a blend of science, culture, and hospitality. From world-class sessions to unforgettable surroundings, this is more than a congress, it’s an experience.
