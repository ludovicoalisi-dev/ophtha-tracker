Annual Meeting
Merci, Montréal!
The 2026 ASRS Annual Meeting may be over, but there's still plenty for members to explore. Thanks to our outstanding Program Chair Audina Berrocal, MD, FASRS, presenters, exhibitors, and attendees, Montréal was one of our most successful meetings ever, with record attendance and abstract submissions.
Enjoy exploring the following meeting content:
ASRS Award winners
(Pyron, Founders, Packo, Young Investigator, Crystal Apple, Image and Chapter of the Year).
Creative Award winners
(See winning content from the Film Festival, Poster Competition and Photo Contest).
Updates From the Field interviews
video interview with select meeting presenters.
Retina Times
e-News Brief
summarizing key takeaways from each day of the scientific program.
The
meeting app
is available to attendees for 2 months after the meeting. Content will be added to the
Annual Meeting Archives
later this year.
Watch for our
2026 Meeting Photo Gallery
coming soon.
A big thanks to our volunteer reporting team for capturing and sharing the moments, insights, and innovations that made the 44th Annual Scientific Meeting in Montréal simply unforgettable.
Mark your calendar for our 45th ASRS Annual Scientific Meeting, July 28-31 in San Francisco. Abstract submissions will open on December 3, 2026 and close on January 28, 2027.
We look forward to seeing you there!
