In memory of Professor Erik Greve
The European Glaucoma Society mourns the passing of
Professor Erik Greve
, a defining figure in the history of the EGS
and one of the key figures in the Society’s creation.
EGS President from 1988 to 2000, he helped shape the Society and foster international
collaboration. His vision of an open and collaborative glaucoma community will remain
an important part of the EGS legacy.
Luis Abegão Pinto, EGS President

In memory of Professor Antonio Fea
It is with profound sadness that we share the news of the passing of
Professor Antonio Fea
, a deeply valued member of the
European Glaucoma Society (EGS) for many decades.
Antonio was not only an outstanding colleague but also a dear friend to many.
He will be remembered for his warmth, infectious sense of humour, and the joyful
energy he brought to every gathering.
Luis Abegão Pinto, EGS President
